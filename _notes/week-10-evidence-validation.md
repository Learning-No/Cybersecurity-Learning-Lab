## Week overview
Good documentation lets another authorized reviewer understand what happened without repeating unnecessary risk. Collect only what is needed and protect sensitive material.

## Learning objectives
- Distinguish observation, interpretation, and assumption.
- Record steps, timestamps, affected components, and expected behavior.
- Remove sensitive details before sharing notes.

## Concepts to learn
- **Reproducibility:** another learner can follow the documented steps.
- **Minimal proof:** capture enough to support a claim, not unrelated content.
- **Sanitization:** remove passwords, tokens, personal data, and identifiers.
- **Evidence handling:** record how material was collected, stored, and accessed.
- **Retest:** repeat the original safe check after a fix.

## Guided safe exercise
1. Use a fictional finding or a lab-provided challenge result.
2. Write the setup and preconditions needed to reproduce it.
3. Separate observed output from your interpretation of impact.
4. Create a mock screenshot or text excerpt using dummy values.
5. Record the time, lab version, and limitations.
6. Store material in a private location approved for the exercise.

## Deliverable
Create a short record containing reproduction steps, sanitized proof, environment details, limitations, and a retest field.

## Self-check
- What makes proof sufficient but not excessive?
- Which values must be removed before sharing a screenshot?
- Why document test limitations?

## Completion checklist
- [ ] My evidence supports the specific claim.
- [ ] I separated facts from interpretation.
- [ ] I removed secrets and personal information.
- [ ] I recorded environment details and limitations.
