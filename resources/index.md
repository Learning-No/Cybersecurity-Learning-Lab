<section class="section"><div class="section-head"><div><p class="eyebrow">HOW TO READ NEWS LIKE AN ANALYST</p><h2>Use a repeatable news checklist</h2></div><p>Headlines often simplify technical details. Check the original advisory before drawing conclusions.</p></div><div class="process-cards"><article><span>01</span><h3>Find the primary source</h3><p>Locate the vendor advisory, CVE record, researcher report, or official incident statement.</p></article><article><span>02</span><h3>Confirm scope</h3><p>Which versions, configurations, and environments are affected? What is explicitly not affected?</p></article><article><span>03</span><h3>Assess relevance</h3><p>Is the issue exploited in the wild? Does the system you administer use the affected component?</p></article><article><span>04</span><h3>Record the action</h3><p>Patch, mitigate, monitor, or document why the issue does not apply. Use a change process.</p></article></div></section>
